# FHNW Semester 1, Group Project, Projet idea: Study planner

## Our Idea / Description 
A simple Programm that helps us students to organize our study time. 
User needs to enter the subjects, dates of the exam and the available time. 
Part of the calculation will be based on ECTS.

## Flow 
- in -> subjects, exam dates, ECTS and personal available time
- happens -> calculation of study times 
- out -> the study plan for semester
